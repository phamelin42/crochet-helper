Mabel Market Bag
By
Blue Star Crochet
Posted:
August 28, 2025

This free pattern—complete with a step-by-step video tutorial—features a breathable lace mesh design made with worsted-weight cotton and a 6.5 mm hook. Beginning with a versatile oval-shaped foundation chain, you can fully customize the bag's width by adjusting your chain stitches, and tailor its height by working additional rounds. The lace body is built from simple yet elegant standing double crochets and V-stitches, creating a sturdy yet airy texture perfect for holding produce or beach essentials.

Once you've reached the desired size, two quick rounds of single crochet secure the top edge, followed by squishy puff-stitch handles. These handles can be made short for handheld use or lengthened to create a comfortable shoulder strap—ideal for balancing practicality and style.

The finished bag measures approximately 40 cm (16") in length and 38 cm (15") in width (excluding handles), though customization is encouraged. Suitable for confident beginners, this pattern offers clear instructions, stitch diagrams, and a video walkthrough. The combination of breathable lace fabric, eco-friendly materials, and customizable sizing makes it an excellent project for handmade gifting or sustainable living.
