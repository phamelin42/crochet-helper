Lantern Table Mat
Posted:

Recherche

This free pattern, with a short video tutorial, shows how to make a round table mat with a scalloped edge. It starts from a magic ring and grows outward in cotton yarn, so the mat lies flat under a teapot or a plant pot. You can stop earlier for a coaster or keep going for a placemat.

The finished mat measures about 22 cm (8.5") across. Suitable for beginners who already know the double crochet. The pattern includes stitch diagrams and a photo for every stage.
