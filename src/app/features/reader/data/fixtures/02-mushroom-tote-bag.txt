Acorn Pouch Free Crochet Pattern
Inspired by the classic granny square
Materials:
• US E/4 crochet hook
• Tapestry needle
• Stitch marker
• Sport weight cotton yarn (I'll list the 
colors I used in parentheses)
o 2 oz cream (the plain one from 
my stash)
o 1.5 oz brown
o Small amount of:
§ gold (leftover from a 
scarf)
§ olive (leftover from a 
hat)
§ rust (a very old ball 
from a swap)
Petals (make 8):
Start with gold.
Round 1: Into a magic ring, chain 3, dc 7, sl st to top of ch 3 [not the first dc!], pull ring closed. (8)
Round 2: Ch 1, 2 sc in each stitch around, sl st to first sc (16)
Round 3: Ch 1, sc in same stitch, sc inc, *sc 2, sc inc* around, sl st to first sc (24)
Round 4: Ch 1, sc in each stitch around, sl st to first sc (24)
[This is how I avoid a visible seam when I work in the round – if you 
have another method you like, use it! Only be sure the first round has 8 stitches, and 
that after the 4th round you have 24 stitches.]
Change to cream.
