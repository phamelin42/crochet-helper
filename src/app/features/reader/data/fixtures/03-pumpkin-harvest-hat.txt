Little 
Acorn Hat
Size: 3-6 months
Materials:
• DK Weight 
Yarn in Brown and 
Cream - (Shown in 
Soft Cotton colors Chestnut 
and Ivory)
• E Hook
Abbreviations:
sc= single crochet
dc = double crochet
hdc= half double crochet
BLO= back loop only
sl st= slip stitch
ch= chain
dec= decrease
Pompon
With Cream Yarn, Chain 2
Row 1: In 2nd Chain from hook, make 5 sc's. Do not join with a slip stitch.
You'll be working in a spiral until the last row.
Row 2: 2 sc in each of the next 5 sc's. Now you should
have a small flat circle. If it curls, pull the starting
tail a bit to flatten it, then keep going without
turning.
Row 3: sc in each of the next 10 sc's.
Row 4: sc in each of the next 10 sc's.
Row 5 (Decrease row): sc2tog 5 times. Stuff the ball lightly.
Row 6: sc2tog 2 times, sc in the next sc.
NOW we can slip stitch to the next sc.
Row 7: Ch 1, sc in same stitch, sc in each remaining sc. Join with
slip stitch to the first sc. Finish off (5 sc's made) Little Acorn Hat
Pattern for personal use only 2
Hat
Row 1: Using Brown, join with slip stitch to any sc from Row 7 of the pompon. Chain 2, hdc in the same
sc, *2 hdc in the next sc, repeat from *4 more times, join with slip stitch into beginning ch2 (10
hdc's and 1 ch2 made)
Row 2: Ch2, hdc in same stitch, 2 hdc in the next hdc, *hdc in the next hdc, 2 hdc in the next hdc, repeat
from * 3 more times, join with slip stitch into beginning ch2 (15 hdc's and 1 ch2 made)
Row 3: Ch2, hdc in the next 2 hdc's, 2 hdc in the same hdc, *hdc in the next 2 hdc's, , 2 hdc in
the next hdc, repeat from * 3 more times, join with slip stitch into beginning
ch2 (20 hdc's and 1 ch2 made)
Rows 4-9: Ch2, hdc in the next 19 hdc's, join with slip stitch into beginning ch2 (20 hdc's and 1
ch2 made)
Rows 10-12: Ch1, sc in next 20 stitches, join with slip stitch to beginning sc.
Row 13: Ch1, sc BLO in the previous 20 stitches. Join with slip stitch to beginning sc. Finish off.
Bow and Ties
Using Cream, Chain 9
Row 1: sc in 2nd chain from hook and the remaining 7 stitches and turn
Row 2: Ch2, hdc in the same stitch, hdc in the next 3 stitches, 2 sc's in the last
stitch and turn. Working back the other side, do the opposite. Hdc in the next 3 stitches, hdc in the next
stitch and then ch2 and slip stitch to the last stitch.
Chain 25, turn, 2 sc's in the 2nd chain from hook. 2 sc's in all the remaining stiches until you're back at
the base of the bow. Finish off.
Now sew the bow to the brim however you want it. I like to add a stitch through the middle
to hold the bow flat and then fasten the ties on the inside as well.
