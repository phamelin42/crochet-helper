Pumpkin 
Harvest Hat
Size: 6-12 months
Materials:
• Worsted Weight 
Yarn in Orange and 
Green - (Shown in 
Red Heart Super 
Saver colors Carrot 
and Frosty Green)
• G Hook
Abbreviations:
sc= single crochet
dc = double crochet
tr= treble crochet
hdc= half double crochet
FPDC= front post double 
crochet
ch= chain
RSC= reverse single crochet
Stem
With Green Yarn, Chain 2
Row 1: In 2nd Chain from hook, make 4 sc's. Do not slip stitch into 
the next stitch. You'll be working in a spiral until the last row.
Row 2: sc in each of the next 4 sc's. At this point, it may be trying 
to turn inside out, to keep the right side showing, make sure you 
turn it right side out before continuing too far. What helps me is to 
make sure I'm crocheting the stem "away" from me, not towards 
you.
Row 3: sc in each of the next 4 sc's.
Row 4: sc in each of the next 4 sc's.
Row 5 (Increase row): 2 sc in the next sc, sc in the next stitch, 2 sc 
in the next sc, sc in the next stitch.
Row 6: sc in each of the next 6 sc's.
NOW we can slip stitch to the next sc.
Row 7: Ch 3, dc in same stitch, 2 dc's in the next 5 sc's. Join with 
slip stitch to the beginning chain 3. Finish off (11 dc's and 1 Ch3

Hat
Row 1: Using Orange, Join with slip stitch to any dc from Row 7 of the stem. Chain 3, FPDC in the same 
DC, *(dc, FPDC) in the next dc, repeat from *10 more times, join with slip stitch into beginning ch3 (12 
dc's, 12 FPDC's and 1 ch3 made)
Row 2: Ch3, dc in same stitch, FPDC in the FPDC of previous round, *2 dc in next dc, FPDC in the FPDC of 
previous round, repeat from * 10 more times, join with slip stitch into beginning ch3 (23 dc's, 12 FPDC's 
and 1 ch3 made)
Row 3: Ch3, dc in the next dc, dc in the next FPDC, FPDC in the same FPDC, *dc in the next 2 dc's, , dc in 
the next FPDC, FPDC in the same FPDC, repeat from * 10 more times, join with slip stitch into beginning 
ch3 (35 dc's, 12 FPDC's and 1 ch3 made)
Rows 4-10: Ch3, dc in the next 2 dc's, FPDC in the next FPDC, * dc in the next 3 dc's, FPDC in the next 
FPDC, repeat from the * 10 more times, join with slip stitch into beginning ch3 (35 dc's, 12 FPDC's and 1 
ch3 made)
Rows 11-14: Ch1, sc in next 48 stitches, join with slip stitch to beginning sc.
Row 15: Ch1, RSC in the previous 48 stitches. Join with slip stitch to beginning RSC. Finish off.
Leaf and Vine
Using Green, Chain 7
Row 1: sc in 2nd chain from hook and the remaining 5 stitches and turn
Row 2: Ch3, Tr in the same stitch, dc in the next 2 stitches, hdc in the next 2 stitches, 3 sc's in the last 
stitch and turn. Working back the other side, do the opposite. Hdc in the next 2 stitches, dc in the next 
2 stitches, Tr in the next stitch and then ch3 and slip stitch to the last stitch. 
Chain 30, turn, 2 sc's in the 2nd chain from hook. 2 sc's in all the remaining stiches until you're back at 
the base of the leaf. Finish off.
Now sew the leaf and vine to your stem however you want it. I actually did a stitch from the leaf to the 
stem to help the leaf stand up and then connected the vine on bottom and secured it there as well.
