Pattern Tea bag holder
1. 8sc in Magic Ring (8)
2. 2sc in each sc around (16)
3. *1sc, 2sc in next*repeat 8x (24)
4. 1sc in BLO around (24)
5-14. 1sc in each sc (both loops)(24)
You can change color here if you want
15-20. 1sc in each sc (24)
21-26. 8sc, chain 1, turn (8)
27. 8sc (8)
28. chain 8 and join it to the other side with a slip stich.
29. Now crochet 12 sc around the chain, finish off and sew the
yarn into the crocheted tea bag holder.
Careful, after round 16 it is the best timing to get your charm attached because now you can hide
the yarn neatly.
