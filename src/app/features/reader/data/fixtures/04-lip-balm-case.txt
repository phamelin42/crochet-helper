Pattern Lip balm case
1. 6sc in Magic Ring (6)
2. 2sc in each sc around (12)
3. *2sc, 2sc in next*repeat 4x (16)
4. 1sc in BLO around (16)
5-17. 1sc in each sc (both loops)(16)
You can switch color if you want
18-25. 1sc in each sc (16)
26-33. 6sc, chain 1, turn (6)
34. 6sc (6)
35. chain 6 and attach it to the other side with a slip stich.
36. Now crochet 10 sc around the chain, finish off and weave the 
yarn into the crocheted lip balm case. 
Beware, after round 19 it is the best timing to get your button attached because now you can work 
away the yarn neatly.
