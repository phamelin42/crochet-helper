Abbreviations (US)
st - stich
rnd(s' - round(s)
sc - single crochet
mr - magic ring
inc - increase (2sc in 1 st)
dec - decrease (sc 2 together)
ch - chain
slst - slipstitch
sk - skip
hdc - half double crochet
Acorn Charm Keychain - Pattern for small scraps
Acorn Body
for a green acorn in olive, for a ripe acorn in tan:
[_] 1 - 6sc in mr
[_] 2 - (inc)x6 (12)
[_] 3 - (sc, inc)x6 (18)
[_][_] 4-5 - sc around (18) (2rnds)
[_] 6 - (sc, dec)x6 (12)
[_][_][_][_][_][_] 7-12 - sc around (12) (6 rnds - or until the pebble is covered so far that you can still take it out)
sew a small brown button on the side opposite to where your working yarn is
ch25+X, slst in Xth st from hook to create a loop for the ring. adjust X to fit your ring. hide your yarn end in the chain.
Acorn Cap
for a green acorn in dark green, for a ripe acorn in brown:
[_] 1 - 6sc in mr
[_] 2 - (inc)x6 - (12)
[_] 3 - sc around (12)
[_] 4 - (sc, inc)x6 (18)
[_] 5 - sc around (18)
[_] 6 - (sc, inc, sc)x6 (24)
[_] 7 - (sc3, inc)x6 (30)
[_] 8 - crab st around (30)
FO and weave in ends
Leaves - for the Green Acorn (leave out for Ripe Acorn)
in light green:
small - sc3 in mr, slst in 1st sc, FO
medium - sc5 in mr, slst in 1st sc, FO
large - hdc5 in mr, slst in 1st hdc, FO
make 2 of each size and sew onto the cap.
Assembly
fit the cap over the body between rounds 3&4, going in through the top and then back out through the bottom on the opposite side. slide the loop through the ring and you're done! pulling the last stitch tight, you can thread the chain around a zipper pull for example.
