Abbreviations (US)
st - stich
rnd(s' - round(s)
sc - single crochet
mr - magic ring
inc - increase (2sc in 1 st)
dec - decrease (sc 2 together)
ch - chain
slst - slipstitch
crab st - crab stitch aka reverse single crochet
hdc - half double crochet
Lipbalm Mushroom Bag-/Keychain - Pattern by JoCupckake
Mushroom Stem
for toadstool in white, for brown mushroom in beige:
[_] 1 - 6sc in mr
[_] 2 - (inc)x6 (12)
[_] 3 - (sc, inc)x6 (18)
[_][_] 4-5 - sc around (18) (2rnds)
[_] 6 - (sc, dec)x6 (12)
[_][_][_][_][_][_][_][_][_][_] 7-16 - sc around (12) (10 rnds - or until your chapstick is covered so far that you can still take it out)
sew a small white or beige button on the opposite side of the mushroom from where your working yarn is
ch30+X, slst in Xth st from hook to create a loop for the button. adjust X to fit your button. hide your yarn end in the chain.
Mushroom Cap
for toadstool in red, for brown mushroom in brown:
[_] 1 - 6sc in mr
[_] 2 - (inc)x6 - (12)
[_] 3 - sc around (12)
[_] 4 - (sc, inc)x6 (18)
[_] 5 - sc around (18)
[_] 6 - (sc, inc, sc)x6 (24)
[_] 7 - (sc3, inc)x6 (30)
[_] 8 - (sc2, inc, sc2)x6 (36)
[_] 9 - (sc5, inc)x6 (42)
[_] 10 - crab st around (42)
FO and weave in ends
Mushroom Spots - for Toadstool (leave out for Brown Mushroom)
in white:
small - sc4 in mr, slst in 1st sc, FO
medium - sc6 in mr, slst in 1st sc, FO
large - hdc6 in mr, slst in 1st hdc, FO
make 2 of each size and sew onto the cap.
Assembly
thread the chain through the cap between rounds 4&5, going up through from the bottom and then back down through from the top on the opposite side. put the loop around 
the button and you're done! unthreading the last pull-through, you can thread the chain around the handle of a tote bag for example.
