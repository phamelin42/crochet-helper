Little Bear Amigurumi
Materials:
- 3 mm hook
- DK cotton yarn in brown and cream
- 8 mm safety eyes
- Fiberfill
Abbreviations:
sc - single crochet
inc - increase
dec - decrease
Gauge:
10 sc and 11 rounds = 5 cm
Notes:
The pieces are worked in a spiral, without joining.
[image 1]
Head
[image 2]
Rnd 1: 6 sc in a magic ring (6)
Rnd 2: inc in each st around (12)
Tip: place a stitch marker every 10 rounds so you do not lose count.
Rnd 3: (sc, inc) x 6 (18)
Rnds 4-8: sc in each st around (18)
Make sure the stitches stay tight.
Rnd 9: (sc, dec) x 6 (12)
Fasten off. Stuff the head firmly.
Ear (make 2)
[image 3]
Note: do not stuff the ears.
Rnd 1: 6 sc in a magic ring (6)
Rnd 2: (sc, inc) x 3 (9)
Rnd 3: sc in each st around (9)
Fasten off, leaving a long tail. Sew the ears to the head between rounds 4 and 6.
