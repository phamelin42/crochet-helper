Cozy Coaster Set
Materials: 4 mm hook, 50 g worsted cotton, tapestry needle
Skill level: Beginner
Abbreviations
ch = chain
sl st = slip stitch
dc = double crochet
Gauge
10 dc and 5 rounds = 10 cm
Coaster
Round 1: Ch 4, join with a sl st to form a ring. Ch 3, 11 dc in the ring, join (12)
Round 2: Ch 3, dc in same st, 2 dc in each st around, join (24)
Round 3: Ch 3, 2 dc in next st, *dc in next st, 2 dc in next st; repeat from * around, join (36)
Fasten off and weave in the ends.
Leaf Decoration
Make a chain of 12 stitches, then slip stitch back along the chain, working one half double crochet in the middle stitches.
Fasten off and leave a tail. Sew the leaf to the edge of the coaster.
Tip: use a contrasting colour for the leaf so that it stands out.
