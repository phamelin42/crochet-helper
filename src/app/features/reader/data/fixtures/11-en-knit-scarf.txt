Simple Garter Scarf
Materials:
Worsted weight yarn, 200 g
US 8 (5 mm) needles
Gauge:
18 sts and 36 rows = 4 in
Notes:
Slip the first stitch of every row for a neat edge.
Scarf
Cast on 30 stitches.
Row 1 (RS): K all sts.
Row 2 (WS): K all sts.
Rows 3-100: Repeat rows 1 and 2.
Hint: count your garter ridges instead of rows, one ridge is 2 rows.
Bind off all stitches loosely. Weave in the ends.
Fringe
Cut 40 strands of yarn, each 20 cm long.
Fold two strands in half and attach them to the scarf edge. Repeat every 2 cm.
